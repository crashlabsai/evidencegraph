# Readiness and next use

EvidenceGraph is usable now as an offline evidence-audit prototype and as a
regression target for collector design. This work does not establish that it saves
responders time or improves their real incident decisions. The distinction matters
more than polishing a sprint entry.

| Sprint criterion | Evidence supplied | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|
| Impact and innovation | A reproduced misleading forensic conclusion, corrected default, portable stress cases and evidence requests | Receipt replay is familiar; no claim to a new security primitive or proven operational impact |
| Execution | Native logs and actual registry operations; independent private scoring labels; paired controls; 36 passing contracts | One scripted action structure, three payload seeds, no held-out incident or adversarial collector |
| Presentation | Compact result table, cited dockets, official-template draft and a fifteen-minute walkthrough | No judge, responder or other human has reviewed this pack |
| Track 1 utility | An executable check for overclaiming from contaminated evidence, with sufficient-evidence conditions | Does not prevent containment failures, certify a lab, or demonstrate agent-speed throughput |

## Next bounded study

1. Acquire one consented corpus with native transcripts and genuinely independent
   mutation records. Have a responder establish answerable questions and ground
   truth without seeing the tool's answers. Document missing evidence and collector
   control rather than inferring them from log format.
2. Freeze the analyzer and investigation questions. Compare an investigator using
   ordinary logs/scripts with the same workflow plus EvidenceGraph. Counterbalance
   condition order and use disjoint cases to reduce learning effects.
3. Record wrong supported claims, correct claims, justified abstentions, unresolved
   evidence requests and elapsed investigation time. Keep private truth hidden
   until both conditions finish. A working UI or a lower refusal rate is not the
   outcome measure.
4. Ask a separate investigator to resolve a sample of citations and reproduce the
   result from retained raw evidence. Investigate disagreements before broader use.

Proceed to a larger study only if the graph adds useful decisions or saves measured
time without increasing unjustified conclusions. If ordinary scripts do as well,
retain the collection checks and simplify the investigator surface.
